VaultRun Team & Enterprise

Compliance report

ItemDetail
InputEvidence packs from vaultrun evidence export --all and the public signing key — nothing else, works offline
ChecksEvery pack's Ed25519 signature and RFC 3161 timestamp chain; rejected packs are listed with the reason
ControlsEvidence integrity, independent time, audit log, traceability, kernel isolation, network egress, accountability — each with status, findings and exceptions
OutputSelf-contained HTML (prints to PDF) and JSON, with the SHA-256 of every input file
Automation--fail-on fail|attention exits non-zero, for CI or a monthly job
